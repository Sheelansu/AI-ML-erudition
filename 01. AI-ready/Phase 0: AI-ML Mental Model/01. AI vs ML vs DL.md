# AI vs ML vs DL

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Making machines perform tasks that require human-like intelligence.

**Example:** A chess-playing program.

## 2. Machine Learning (ML)
A subset of AI where machines learn patterns from data instead of relying only on explicitly programmed rules.

**Example:** An email spam detector trained on data.

## 3. Deep Learning (DL)
A subset of ML that uses multilayered neural networks to learn complex patterns.

**Example:** A neural network that recognizes objects in images.

## Relationship

**AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL**

- **AI:** The broad field of intelligent machines.
- **ML:** Learning patterns from data.
- **DL:** Learning complex patterns using deep neural networks.

Modern generative AI systems like ChatGPT rely heavily on deep learning, particularly **transformer neural networks**.
